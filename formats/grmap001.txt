.SH NAME
grmap001 \- Graal gmap text, short form and generator form
.SH DESCRIPTION
A
.I .gmap
is line-oriented text.
The first line is the signature
.BR GRMAP001 .
Two writers in this edition emit it, and they do not emit the same keys.
.SS Short form
.BR dungeongenerator (7)
writes, from
.BR 0x406760 :
.PP
.RS
.nf
GRMAP001
WIDTH <levels across>
HEIGHT <levels down>
GENERATED <bottom-right level name>
.fi
.RE
.PP
There is no
.B LEVELNAMES
block.
The client rebuilds the grid from
.BR GENERATED ,
which names the last level, not the number 1.
Level names are
.I LEVELSTART ,
a column letter, and a 1-based row.
See
.BR dungeongenerator (7).
.SS Generator form
.BR terraingenerator (7)
writes, from
.BR 0x44c5f5 :
.PP
.RS
.nf
GRMAP001
WIDTH <levels>
HEIGHT <levels>
GENERATED 1
GENSEED <seed>
GENBASE <base>
GENEVENBORDERS <0 or 1>
GENHEIGHT <height>
GENCHAOS <chaos>
LEVHEIGHT <per-level height>
LEVCHAOS <per-level chaos>
MAPIMG <basename>.png
<one level name per row>
HEIGHTMAP
<coarse or fine samples>
HEIGHTMAPEND
RANDOMSEEDS
<one seed per level, when present>
RANDOMSEEDSEND
.fi
.RE
.PP
.B GENERATED 1
here is a flag, not a filename.
Level names are basename, column
.I A
then
.IR AA ,
and a zero-padded row, plus
.IR .nw .
.B MAPIMG
is the overview the client shows on the map list.
The PNG the tool saves from the preview dialog is a separate file.
.PP
.B HEIGHTMAP
is the field.
A sibling
.I _heights.txt
is the same numbers without the gmap wrapper, and the
.I .txt
branch of the save dialog writes that file.
Each level file then carries a 9 by 9
.B HEIGHTS
block.
See
.BR glevnw01 (5).
.SS GraalLevelGen
The picture generator writes
.B GRMAP001
with
.B WIDTH
and
.BR HEIGHT ,
then a
.B LEVELNAMES
list of quoted filenames separated by commas.
That is a third shape.
Do not assume
.B GENERATED
means the same thing in all three files.
