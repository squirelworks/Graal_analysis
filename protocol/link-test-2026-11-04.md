# Where real 2.05 tests a level link: a lab measurement

Date: 2026-11-04. Tags: [cap] for what the captures show.

## Setup

- **Client:** real Graal 2.05 rev2 (`GNW03081`) under Wine 9, in a private
  network namespace with loopback only. The exe ran from a copy of the Graal
  Reborn folder.
- **Game server:** the Seihai project's GPL GServer-v2 fork
  (`seihai-gserver-v2`, commit `f585ca6`).
- **Test level:** a generated all-grass level, so no wall or NPC is in the way.
- **Links:** every link is `LINK dest x y w h playerx playery`. When the client
  takes a link, its LEVELWARP (id 0) therefore carries the player's own x and y
  at that moment.
- **Movement:** the player starts from the account position and walks in a
  straight line with one arrow key held down.
- **Decoding:** `seihai capture show <pcap> --version 2.05`. The captures are
  private to the project and hold only a lab password.

Positions are in tiles. The player's x, y is the top-left of the 3×3-tile
sprite box. In these runs 2.05 moved 0.5 tile per step and sent x and y in half
tiles, so each result below is a half-open range 0.5 tile wide.

## Result

| Walking | Point tested along the walk | Point tested across the walk | From runs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| right | x + [2.5, 3) | y + [2, 2.5) | R; Y1 to Y5 |
| left | x + [0, 0.5) | not measured | L |
| down | y + [3, 3.5) | x + [1.5, 2) | D; X1 to X3 |
| up | y + [0.5, 1) | not measured | U |

The client tests one point against the link rectangles, not a body rectangle.
Across the walk, the point is at the player's centre, (x+1.5, y+2). Along the
walk, it sits 1 to 1.5 tiles ahead of that centre. A 1×1 link off the centre
line is walked straight past: rows 31 and 33 in Y1 and Y2, against row 32 in
Y4.

The LEVELWARP from 2.05 carries the destination name lowercased: `linkr.nw`
for a link to `linkR.nw`.

## Not checked

- Diagonal walking.
- Standing still on a link, for example when a warp puts the player on one.
- Links at the level edge.
- Any version other than 2.05 rev2.
