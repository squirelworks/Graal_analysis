# Record seven exact Linux client local-login baselines

Publication prose is derived only from the sanitized owner handoffs listed below. Their file hashes were independently computed during preparation; underlying native observations and original-carrier identities are researcher-supplied. Raw code, native addresses, client assets and private capture payloads are excluded. These source IDs identify handoff documents, not newly materialized raw captures.

remains a separate accepted-login baseline, with its visual limitations intact.

The local server was built from squirelworks/seihai-gserver-v2 commit `1b0a6e15641aa542168d5fd0878517d15d990adc`, with reviewed lab changes to its pinned gs2lib dependency and platform-specific cipher selection. Final executable SHA-256: `97d891fda061811af625c7b2d28b3d55c83c125b1dc23e97f9d6c973041c3743`. A host copy on D was independently hash-verified.

All seven unique original Linux executable identities reached accepted local logins on that final binary:

- 4.04r2 x86: `G3D19026`.
- 4.20r6 and r8 x86: shared `G3D22126`, with distinct executable hashes.
- 6.01r1 x86 and x64: shared `G3D0108A`.
- 6.03r7 x64: `G3D0511C`.

These are actual first-frame observations, not token guesses from another platform. The initial unknown-version rejections and the later accepted reruns are preserved separately. Exact identities and observations are in the adjacent sanitized JSON. Fourteen compiled lookup cases, feature-order/default-profile checks and 272 codec assertions also passed.

The tests use private user/network namespaces, local-only DNS/list fixtures, disposable identities and fresh writable runtime copies. No public service was contacted. All final captures are untruncated, original executable hashes are unchanged, and recorded owned processes were verified stopped. The parent independently rehashed 35 final3.04, 127 final4.x and 74 final6.x collection files after transfer. The three6.x captures retain their sampling limitations, including two historical process-environment inspection omissions whose PIDs were absent at followup.

Accepted login is distinct from a usable game or Login service. Final4.04 rendered a tiled surface without a visibly established avatar. Both4.20 builds and all6.x builds remained black despite accepted backend traffic. An earlier3.04 run rendered the independent level and avatar, while its final neutral regression was black. Resource requests and missing Login/bootstrap behavior are recorded; no sole cause or rendering fix is asserted. These are usable network research baselines, not a claim of seven fully playable clients.

Fresh independent GS1 source-weapon tests on original3.04 separately proved source delivery, created/playerenters marker messages and persistence in a fresh server account. Numeric storage and explicit comparisons worked in further trials, while bare predicates disagreed with the shared model. Appendix E200 now has a separate exact Windows2.05r2 resolution published in PR337; Linux3.04 predicate parsing and broader cross-build semantics remain open. These are GS1 observations, distinct from TorqueScript or GS2.

The native selected-function recovery and exports are complete for all Linux builds, including the verified combined v3 package. The work narrows GS2 reader/dispatch/state-save, class/cache/checksum and delivery contracts; it does not recover the full language or standard library. The class197 envelope and cache-checksum candidates still require controlled runtime delivery tests. Windows-specific E503 is unaffected.
