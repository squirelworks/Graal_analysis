# What Ash is (owner statement, 2026-11-20)

Date: 2026-11-20. Evidence: **[doc]**, the repository owner's statement in the project thread.

## Statement

The owner states that Ash was an early attempt at working out how to reproduce Graal. It is not
Graal: none of the Ash material is an original Graal program, server or file.

## What this means for the notes

- Both bodies of notes that use the name belong to that reproduction effort: the blueprint pack
  in `ash/` (an analysis of the 2006 39ster 2.1 GServer, with the Phoenix rebuild) and the Python
  rewrite of the gs2emu protocol in `server-notes/ash-overview.md`.
- Neither is evidence of how an original Graal client or server behaved. Where the manual cites
  them, they describe a third-party remake (the 39ster tree) or the reproduction attempt itself.
- The different default ports (14812 and 14822), languages and subjects stand. They are parts of
  one effort, not two unrelated projects that happen to share a name.
