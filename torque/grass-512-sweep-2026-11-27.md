# 5.12 grass: what decides whether it draws (T3D-048)

Date: 2026-11-27. Client: the real Windows 5.12, in throw-away copies of the conformance lab
VMs (headless, overlays, VM-only link), against seihai-gserver 833faba. Runner and judge:
[`tools/conformance/`](../tools/conformance/). Derived data only: the screenshots and logs stay
on the lab host.

## The rule

**A 5.12 grows no grass while its control object (the avatar) stands in the world's origin
cell, that is with |x| < 8 and |y| < 8.** The client still creates the ghost. Outside that
cell, grass draws at every radius from 1 to 8 cells. There is no radius threshold. The
radius counts 8-unit cells: radius 1 gives about one clump around the avatar, and a larger
radius adds rows further out, which a ground-level view barely shows. The count a cell and
the scale change only how much ground is covered. **[lab]**

This is the same origin-cell dead spot already known for `DynamicShapeReplicator`
([replicators-512.md](replicators-512.md), conformance C031), and it now covers the grass
classes too.

## Method

Each run started from conformance C036, a raw `DynamicGrass` (`gen_content.grass_rawobject`,
bit for bit the server's replicator encoding) at radius 4, 40 to 60 a cell, scale 3, the
magenta `conf_grass.png`, the replicator and the spawn at (20, 20), on flat streamed terrain.
Each run changed one value and made two sessions with the cache kept. Every run was judged
two ways:

- **diff**: the share of the lower half of the final screenshot that differs from C046, the
  same world with no grass line (tol 40, and 2% or more counts as drawn);
- **magenta**: the share of the lower half that is magenta.

Each screenshot was also checked by eye. A fully covered view caps both measures at about
0.674.

## What each suspicion came to

- **The avatar in the origin cell: confirmed.** Nothing grows at radius 4 or 8, wherever the
  replicator is. One coordinate under 8 is not enough: (9, 4), (4, 20) and (20, 4) all grow.
- **Radius: no threshold.** Radius 1 to 8 all draw, and the near view at 4 and at 8 is the same.
- **Count and scale: coverage only.** 5 a cell at scale 1 covers about 2.5% of the lower half,
  right at the diff check's line. At radius 1 it covers 0.8%, which the check calls "no grass"
  although a clump is plainly there.
- **White grass on white terrain: ruled out.** White grass is shaded grey and shows clearly,
  and the terrain never takes the grass texture or any other one.
- **The replicator's position: no effect** ((0, 0) against (20, 20)).
- **seihai-gserver's radius-8 floor (#218): not needed.** With it removed, a plain radius-4
  line draws.

## What this changes

The earlier "no grass below radius 8" bisection in [replicators-512.md](replicators-512.md)
most likely saw an avatar inside the origin cell, or sparse scale-1 grass that a pixel count
missed. Neither explains a radius-4 line failing where radius 8 drew on the same spawn, and
no variable here reproduced that. Those runs were Wine on Mesa, which this sweep did not
cover, so a Wine/Mesa-only effect is not ruled out **[inf]**.

- seihai-gserver #228 drops the floor (grass goes out at its own radius) and reports a world
  with ground cover whose spawn is in the origin cell.
- Conformance: C035 now expects no floor note, C036 expects grass on 5.12, and the new C047
  checks that grass with the avatar spawned at (4, 4) does not draw.
