**Graal Analysis 0.0.4** is the second edition of the **Graal Technical Reference Manual**: one 377-page PDF, `graal-trm-0.0.4.pdf`. It is built from commit 3a700ad.

0.0.3 had 347 pages. The 30 new pages close the coverage audit of 4 November 2026: every note the audit flagged is now carried in the manual or has a recorded reason in `manual/sources.tsv`.

## How coverage is checked

A new check, `tools/manual/check_carry.py`, runs in CI. A cited note with no recorded reason fails if a section it was planned for does not cite it, or if under 30% of its distinctive terms appear in the manual. 105 cited notes carry such a reason, mostly protocol notes whose facts sit in Parts II and III rather than the Part IV section first planned for them.

## Known gaps

This is not 1.0.0. The roadmap to 1.0.0 is in issue #191.

## Building it yourself

From a checkout of this tag: `python tools/manual/build_manual.py --version 0.0.4`. This needs pandoc and the `playwright` package; see `manual/README.md`.
