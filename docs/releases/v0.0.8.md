**Graal Analysis 0.0.8** is the sixth edition of the **Graal Technical Reference Manual**: one 415-page PDF, `graal-trm-0.0.8.pdf`, built from commit 2dbf2fb. The same edition also comes as a print set for A4 printers, `graal-trm-0.0.8-print-a4.pdf`, with the §0.12 system map as an A3 insert in `graal-trm-0.0.8-a3-inserts.pdf`.

This edition publishes the work merged since 0.0.7, 16 pull requests (#717 to #729 and #747 to #749). Most of it is in two places. Coverage passes over Parts I, II, IV, VIII and X carried the missing tables into the chapters and gave a reason for every other miss, so every Part from 0 to XI now has no unexplained coverage miss. And the last App. E batches settled five entries: Appendix E is down from 32 open entries to 27. The manual is 21 pages longer.

This is the last edition of the current research period. Research is paused after it; `docs/gap-board.md` and `docs/experiments.md` list every open gap and the planned runs, for whoever picks the work up.

## Section numbers that changed

0.0.7 keeps its own numbers for good (VERSIONING.md: each release is frozen). No section of 0.0.7 moved or was renumbered in 0.0.8. The new sections are §4.3.10, §5.3.8, §8.3.11 and §8.4.12, each added after the last subsection of its section. The App. E entries that were settled (E#10, E#18, E#101, E#304 and E#610) are removed from App. E; each answer is in its chapter and listed in §E.12. Entry numbers (E#…) of the questions still open have not changed.

No column of a citable data file changed. `docs/gap-board.tsv` is new: one row per open gap, with where it comes from, its kind, where it can run and its priority.

## How coverage is checked

`tools/manual/coverage.py` reports, per Part, the distinctive terms of each cited note that the manual does not contain. At this build the manual carries 70% of 19,673 terms from 610 cited notes, against 67% of 19,584 terms from 603 notes in 0.0.7. Every miss in Parts 0 to XI and App. G is now either carried or explained in `manual/coverage-explained.tsv`. Only App. E has unexplained misses, 59 of them, mostly sample values from the last lab batches. Carrying or explaining every miss is one of the 1.0.0 criteria in #191, not a condition of this release.

## Known gaps

This is not 1.0.0. The roadmap is in #191, and the research toward 1.0.0 is tracked in #370. GS2 syntax and its standard library (E#207) and the bytes a public 5.x or 6.x `Login` server sends (E#3) are still open.

## Building it yourself

From a checkout of this tag: `python tools/manual/build_manual.py --version 0.0.8`. This needs pandoc and the `playwright` and `pypdf` packages; see `manual/README.md`.
