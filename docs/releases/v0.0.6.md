**Graal Analysis 0.0.6** is the fourth edition of the **Graal Technical Reference Manual**: one 379-page PDF, `graal-trm-0.0.6.pdf`. It is built from commit b0e16ee. As with 0.0.5, the same edition also comes as a print set for A4 printers, `graal-trm-0.0.6-print-a4.pdf`, with the §0.12 system map as an A3 insert in `graal-trm-0.0.6-a3-inserts.pdf`.

This edition publishes the research merged since 0.0.5, about 235 pull requests. That research comes from lab runs on real 2.x to 6.x clients, static reads of the Linux, Mac and Windows images, the Graal 2002 and 1.40 lines, the v7 Worlds clients, and the later Graal3D beta. The manual grew by about 3,500 lines, which is why it is 75 pages longer. Appendix E was rewritten as a discussion of each unknown. Each entry now says what is unknown, the evidence on record, what has been tried, the best current guess, what would settle it, and an outlook (Lab, Read, Owner, Missing or Maybe never). The questions that were answered are kept together at the end, with the reasoning that settled them. Entry numbers have not changed.

The layout is the same as 0.0.5's. Eight wide tables are now printed on A4 landscape pages, against six in 0.0.5.

## How coverage is checked

`tools/manual/coverage.py` reports, per Part, the distinctive terms of each cited note that the manual does not yet contain. At this build the manual carries 70% of 17,144 terms from 561 cited notes, against 71% of 11,600 terms from 374 notes in 0.0.5. The weakest Parts are X (62%) and I (63%). `check_manual.py` and `check_carry.py` still gate every change. The report does not gate CI yet. Carrying or explaining every miss is one of the 1.0.0 criteria in #191, not a condition of this release.

## Known gaps

This is not 1.0.0. The roadmap is in #191, and the research toward 1.0.0 is tracked in #370. GS2 syntax and its standard library (E#207), the Torque-era frame loops (E#301), and the bytes a public 5.x or 6.x `Login` server sends (E#3) are still open.

## Building it yourself

From a checkout of this tag: `python tools/manual/build_manual.py --version 0.0.6`. This needs pandoc and the `playwright` and `pypdf` packages; see `manual/README.md`.
