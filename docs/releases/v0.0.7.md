**Graal Analysis 0.0.7** is the fifth edition of the **Graal Technical Reference Manual**: one 392-page PDF, `graal-trm-0.0.7.pdf`, built from commit eb0cbc1. The same edition also comes as a print set for A4 printers, `graal-trm-0.0.7-print-a4.pdf`, with the §0.12 system map as an A3 insert in `graal-trm-0.0.7-a3-inserts.pdf`.

This edition publishes the work merged since 0.0.6, 21 pull requests (#693 to #714). Most of it is in three places. A new §4.12 covers keys and the mouse, from a key sweep of 2.05r2 to 6.03r7. Fuzzing of the clients, servers, RC and NC is carried into the chapters. And Appendix E is down from 116 open entries to 34: the answered questions moved into the chapters that use them. The manual grew by about 350 lines, and it is 13 pages longer.

## How coverage is checked

`tools/manual/coverage.py` reports, per Part, the distinctive terms of each cited note that the manual does not contain. At this build the manual carries 71% of 18,287 terms from 601 cited notes, against 70% of 17,144 terms from 561 notes in 0.0.6. New in this release, a miss can be explained in `manual/coverage-explained.tsv`. Parts 0, III, V, VI, VII, IX, XI and App. G have no unexplained misses. Parts I, II, IV, VIII, X and App. E still have 4,598 between them. Carrying or explaining every miss is one of the 1.0.0 criteria in #191, not a condition of this release.

## Known gaps

This is not 1.0.0. The roadmap is in #191, and the research toward 1.0.0 is tracked in #370. GS2 syntax and its standard library (E#207) and the bytes a public 5.x or 6.x `Login` server sends (E#3) are still open.

## Building it yourself

From a checkout of this tag: `python tools/manual/build_manual.py --version 0.0.7`. This needs pandoc and the `playwright` and `pypdf` packages; see `manual/README.md`.
