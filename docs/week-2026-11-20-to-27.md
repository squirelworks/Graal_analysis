## Findings to build on

- **Grass:** a 5.12 grows no grass while the avatar stands in the world's origin cell
  (|x| < 8, |y| < 8); elsewhere every radius from 1 to 8 draws. The earlier radius-8 floor was an
  encoding slip in our own lab worlds. [grass sweep](../torque/grass-512-sweep-2026-11-27.md),
  [replicators](../torque/replicators-512.md) **[lab]**
- **5.12 ceilings** are in the binary: 4,096 ghost slots, one 256 by 256 terrain grid, terrain
  requested once and cached per server name, lighting only at Phase 2, a draw horizon of about
  1,000 units (2,400 at most). The server works around them (scoping, sliding window, Phase 2
  again); only a new client lifts them. [plan §1](../torque/3d-next-client-server-plan-2026-11-27.md) **[lab]**
- **Terrain lighting:** hold Phase 2 until terrain, water and Sun are in, and send it again after
  a world switch. [T3D-162](../torque/3d-worldswitch-terrain-lighting-512-2026-11-26.md) **[lab]**
- **4.20r8 terrain** needs `.png` names, is never shaded, grows no cover. [terrain-512](../torque/terrain-512.md) **[lab]**
- **Sun flare** (`fxSunLight`): layout decoded and accepted, but no flare has drawn yet.
  [fxsunlight-512](../torque/fxsunlight-512.md) **[lab]**
- **Open:** up close (within about 70 units) a 5.12 draws terrain in its first texture only, with
  the other materials fading in by about 150 units; the client's renderer, not the data, is the
  suspect. A world switch to a world with no terrain, water or Sun freezes the 5.12 on a sky-only
  frame. **[lab]**

## Versioning of new 3D features

New features ride on the opt-in `seihai3d/1` extension. A real 5.12 never asks for it and gets the
stock protocol byte for byte; Seihai's client asks and falls back to stock when a server does not
answer. The version number lives in seihai-core. Indoors to outdoors should be seamless on the
extension, with the stock world switch kept for real clients.
