# Spin and laying items on the wire (2.05 rev 2 and 2.22)

A loopback lab of the real Windows 2.05 rev 2 client (token `GNW03081`) and the real 2.22 client (token `GNW03014`), both under Wine, on a lab GServer-v2 beta4 with the lab patches. The game server and the decrypting proxy used the lab ports. The level was plain grass, 64 by 64, with no NPCs. The player started with 40 rupees, sword power 1 and full hearts, at tile (30, 30). Outgoing 2.05 frames were re-inflated from the loopback capture and matched the proxy's decode. **[lab][cap]**

The pack this was read from also holds the 2026-11-14 open-issues lab, the GS1-per-build lab, the u7 follow-ups, the Mac PowerPC static read, and several handoff checklists. Those are already notes on main, or they are checklists, and they are not written again here.

## Spin: a second S during the swing

The first S sends `PLI_PLAYERPROPS` with prop 10 (`GANI`) set to `sword`. A second S inside the window sends `GANI` `spin`. On these frames the value is a length byte equal to the name's length, then the name: 5 for `sword`, 4 for `spin` and `idle`. **[lab][cap]**

Second-press timing, measured from the first key-down. Each key was held 50 ms. Twenty-two tries on 2.05 rev 2:

| Second S | Result |
|---|---|
| +91 ms, +93 ms | `sword` only |
| +111 ms to +290 ms | `spin` in 19 of 20 tries |
| +271 ms, once | a second `sword` |
| +131 ms, once | nothing sent |
| +321 ms and later | a new `sword` |

The `spin` prop left 10–50 ms after the second key-down. The usable window in this run was about 0.1–0.3 s after the swing started. The edges moved by about one frame under Wine. **[lab][cap]**

Releasing S sent nothing. Across the swings and spins in this run, `GANI` returned to `idle` only when the player walked, or when A was pressed with S held (the lay pick below). The end of a swing and the end of a spin sent no `idle`. **[lab][cap]**

2.22, three tries at +131, +200 and +271 ms, sent `spin` each time. 2.22 then sent `idle` 630–680 ms after the spin, and after a plain swing as well (`sword` at about +25 ms, `idle` at about +262 ms). The swing also asked the server for `sword.wav` the first time. **[lab][cap]**

## Laying: S held, then A

Facing south on 2.05 rev 2. Times are from the S key-down.

| Time | Keys | Sent |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | S down | `GANI` `sword` |
| 0.804 s | A, S still held | `GANI` `idle`. A green rupee (item 0) is drawn over the head |
| 2.064 s | A, S still held | nothing. The rupee turns blue (item 1) |
| 3.301 s | A, S still held | nothing. The rupee turns red (item 2) |
| 4.658 s | S up | nothing. The red rupee stays over the head |
| 5.704 s | A | `PLI_ITEMADD` (12), then own props with `RUPEESCOUNT` (prop 3) = 10, in one frame |
| 8.62 s after the lay | — | `PLI_ITEMDEL` (13) |

`PLI_ITEMADD` is first, then `RUPEESCOUNT`. The landing for a player at (30, 31) facing south was (30.5, 34), which is `(x + 0.5, y + 3)`. From the first A until 1 s after the lay, the only client-to-server messages were that `idle` and the item frame. Red took 30 rupees (40 down to 10). **[lab][cap]**

One green-rupee lay in each direction. Each S down sent `sword`, and each A with S held sent `idle`. The same frame order held. `PLI_ITEMDEL` followed 8.2–8.6 s later.

| Facing | Player | Landing | Rupees left |
|---|---|---|---|
| north (sprite 0) | (30, 30) | (30.5, 28) | 9 |
| west (1) | (29.5, 30) | (28, 31) | 8 |
| south (2) | (29.5, 30.5) | (30, 33.5) | 7 |
| east (3) | (30, 30.5) | (32.5, 31.5) | 6 |

Each landing is `(x + 0.5 + dx, y + 1 + dy)` with `(dx, dy)` north `(0, −3)`, west `(−2, 0)`, south `(0, +2)`, east `(+2, 0)`. **[lab][cap]**

An arrow while a rupee is held over the head leaves lay mode and sends nothing. Two tries: Up with S still held, and Left after S was released. Neither turned the player nor stepped, and the following A sent no `PLI_ITEMADD`. The rupee count stayed at 6. **[lab][cap]**

## 2.22, one south lay

Same account state, reset to 40 rupees, at (30, 30) facing south. The server was restarted between the two sessions.

| Time from S down | Keys | Sent |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | S down | `GANI` `sword`, a file request for `sword.wav`, then `GANI` `idle` at +262 ms |
| 0.801 s | A, S held | a file request for `item2.wav`. Green rupee over the head. No `GANI`: already `idle` |
| 1.894 s | A, S held | nothing. Blue |
| 2.994 s | A, S held | nothing. Red |
| 4.180 s | S up | nothing |
| 5.182 s | A | `PLI_ITEMADD` then `RUPEESCOUNT` 10, in one frame |
| 8.45 s after the lay | — | `PLI_ITEMDEL` |

The landing was (30.5, 33.5) for a player at (30, 30.5) facing south, again `(x + 0.5, y + 3)`. The item bytes match 2.05. 2.22 asked for `item2.wav` on the pick. It did not ask for `item.wav` on the lay, and no other sound name went out with the lay. 2.05's sounds were not heard: that Wine session had no audio device, and 2.05 did not open a wav file. **[lab][cap]**
