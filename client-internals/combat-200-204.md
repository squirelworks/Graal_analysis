# Combat in 2.00 to 2.04 (victim's client)

How the 2.00, 2.01, 2.02, 2.03 and 2.04 clients judge a sword hit and the hurt packet, compared with the 2.05 rev 2 rules in `decomp-findings-2026-10-24-d/` and `damage-sources.md`. Read in the binaries of 2.00 (rev 0), 2.01, 2.02, 2.03 and 2.04 (MD5s in `build-catalogue.md`), each run through the same Ghidra pipeline as `build-diff-204-205.md` (11.4.2, Delphi compiler spec). The 2.00 revs 1 to 3 and the 2.02 revs 1 and 2 were not read.

Cleaned note: no addresses, no decompiler output, no generated names. Nothing here was seen on a wire or in the lab.

## Short answer

All five builds have a sword hit test and a damage source. None of them is without combat.

- **2.04 is 2.05 rev 2.** The sword judge, the reach box, the hurt routine, the can-be-hurt test, the heart change, the hurt packet handler (ids 40 and 46), the sword-power getter and the no-kill flag test are the same code as 2.05 rev 2. Only the field offsets move (the class layout changes listed in `build-diff-204-205.md`). The 2.05 rules apply to 2.04 as they stand.
- **2.03 differs from 2.04 in three places**: the no-kill flag sits on the player, not the level; the hurt packet has no per-player field test; and neither does the props path that leads to the sword judge.
- **2.01 and 2.02 differ from 2.03 in one more place**: the sword power is stored without the offset and limit, so it is 0 to 63 and never negative.
- **2.00 is the oldest shape.** No kick test, no shared can-be-hurt test in the sword judge, no `playerhurt` event, no id 46, and a hurt packet (40) with only the hurtable, guild and range gates.

## The same in every build (2.00 to 2.05 rev 2)

- **Who decides.** The victim's client judges another player's swing from that player's props, as in 2.05.
- **Swing.** The attacker's animation step is 9 to 13 (sprite S 6 to 10).
- **Reach box.** The victim must be 0 to 4 tiles ahead of the attacker along its facing (both ends count) and strictly under 2 tiles to the side, measured between the two players' X/Y props with the same (1.5, 2) centre offset. The box test is skipped while the victim is in hurt mode (mode 3). The code is the same in all six builds.
- **Damage.** The attacker's sword power, as the victim last received it in SWORDPOWER (prop 8), in half hearts.
- **Wire form of prop 8.** A byte below 10 is the power (1 to 4 are the default swords, with their images). From 10 the power is the byte minus 30, followed by the image name.
- **Victim gates in the sword judge.** Victim AP not exactly 100; the attacker's `(tag)` not in the victim's nickname (the same bracket test as 2.05); two attacker flags clear, the same two flags 2.05 tests, which `combat.md` maps to STATUS 0x02 and 0x20 (that the 2.00 to 2.04 flags carry the same STATUS bits is inferred from their place in the test).
- **Hurt mode.** Mode 3 for 40 ticks (2 s), started for every accepted hit, with the knockback vector normalised.
- **Sword knockback direction.** Where the decompile keeps the box test's call arguments (2.00 and 2.02), the knockback handed to the hurt routine is the victim's centre minus the attacker's point, halved; the hurt routine then normalises it to length 1 (straight down when it is zero). In 2.01, 2.03, 2.04 and 2.05 rev 2 the decompile drops those arguments, so they were not compared.
- **Heart change.** A heal (negative change) needs AP at or above the same threshold expression as 2.05 (40). The paths that block it (swimming, a paused or editor state outside a sparring level) read the same.
- **Hurt packet (40).** Knockback `(byte - 0x60) / 16` tiles, power `byte - 32` half hearts, capped at one heart, and the attacker within 10 tiles (squared distance at most 100).
- **`noplayerkilling`** is in the GS1 command table of every build, and each build has a no-kill test in the sword judge (where it lives differs, below).

Notes on the table:

- **2.00's victim test.** 2.00's sword judge does not call the routine the other paths use. It requires the play state to be exactly 0, so a player in a sparring level with another state is not hit by a sword, and there is no separate paused/editor test. The hurt routine it calls does have the sparring exception.
- **The power store.** The per-player value is kept in a 6-bit slot in every build. In 2.00 to 2.02 the received power goes into it unchanged, so a power below 0 or above 63 does not survive (a wire byte of 10 to 29 gives a negative power). In 2.03 and later the power is limited to -20..20 and stored plus 20. This is the store; how a real 2.00 to 2.02 attacker's negative power looks to a victim was not seen.
- **No-kill.** In 2.00 to 2.03 the command sets a flag on the local player that is cleared on level load, and the sword judge and the hurt packet test it. In 2.04 and 2.05 the flag is on the level (and is read through a tamper-checked byte). Both end when a new level loads, so they behave the same for one player in one level.
- **Once per second.** In the builds read here the per-attacker second-of-day stamp is in the hurt packet path. It was not found in the sword judge of any build (2.00 to 2.05 rev 2). `combat.md` puts it on the sword; that placement was not confirmed by this read.

## Confirmed, inferred, not tested

- Confirmed from the program: everything in the table and in "The same in every build", except the two items marked inferred.
- Inferred: that the two attacker flags are STATUS 0x02 and 0x20 in 2.00 to 2.04; that a negative power wraps in 2.00 to 2.02 (from the store's bit mask).
- Not read: 2.00 revs 1 to 3, 2.02 revs 1 and 2, 1.42 and earlier.
- Not tested in the lab or on a wire.
